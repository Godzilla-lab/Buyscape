# Partnership emails for Nigerian data

Drafts for asking Nigerian companies for data access. Replace everything in
square brackets before sending. Nothing here has been sent.

## What every email asks for

- **Small and safe first.** A sample of 200 to 1,000 records, not the whole database.
- **No personal data.** No names, phone numbers or addresses. We remove them anyway.
- **Something back.** Early access to the benchmark results, credit in the
  write-up, and free use of the valuation API during the pilot.
- **Their choice of terms.** A short data-sharing agreement, under the Nigeria
  Data Protection Act 2023, that they approve.

## Who to send to, in order

| # | Company | What they have | Why it matters |
|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | BuyScrap Nigeria | Past deals: photos, item, final price paid | Settled prices, the rarest data there is |
| 2 | Scrapays, Pakam, Ecobarter | Weights and prices for paper, plastic and metal lots | Truth for the material tier |
